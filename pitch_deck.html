Slide 1: Text Prediction App

Pitching to Investors

This app predicts the next word you will type, saving you time on mobile devices and improving typing efficiency.

Slide 2: The Algorithm

We used a Natural Language Processing (NLP) algorithm based on N-grams (combinations of words) to predict the most likely next word based on the user's input phrase.

Slide 3: The App Interface

Our Shiny app features a simple text box. You type a phrase, press submit, and within 1 second, the algorithm returns the prediction.

Slide 4: How It Works

The app cleans your input text, searches our database of millions of Twitter and news phrases, and returns the highest probability word.
